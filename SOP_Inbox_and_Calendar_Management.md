# 📧 SOP: Executive Inbox & Calendar Management

## 1. Objective
Establish a structured routine for daily inbox zero maintenance, message categorization, and calendar scheduling to protect executive focus.

## 2. Daily Inbox Triage Protocol (Morning & Evening)
1. **Filter Spam & Bulk:** Automatically file newsletters, notifications, and system alerts into designated folders.
2. **Apply 4D Decision Matrix:**
   - **Delete/Archive:** Irrelevant or completed messages.
   - **Delegate:** Forward to appropriate team members with clear instructions.
   - **Do (< 2 mins):** Respond immediately if action takes less than two minutes.
   - **Defer:** Label as `Action Required` or `Pending Review` and schedule time on calendar.

## 3. Calendar Scheduling Rules
- **Buffer Time:** Always place a 15-minute buffer between back-to-back virtual meetings.
- **Meeting Inclusions:** Ensure every calendar invitation contains:
  - Concise Meeting Title
  - Video Link (Google Meet / Zoom)
  - Clear Agenda / Supporting Documents
