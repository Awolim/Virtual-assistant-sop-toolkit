# 🔒 SOP: Client Data Privacy & POPIA Compliance

## 1. Objective
Ensure all physical and digital client records (legal files, medical data, financial logs) are handled with absolute confidentiality and in compliance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

## 2. Security Protocols
- **Password Hygiene:** Store and share client credentials strictly using encrypted password management tools. Never send passwords in plain text via chat or email.
- **Access Control:** Grant document access on a "Least Privilege" basis using Google Drive / Workspace permissions (`Viewer` or `Commenter` by default; `Editor` only when required).
- **File Archiving:** Retain medical and legal imaging records in encrypted digital archives with restricted view access.
